## Nationwide re-tabulation (builds 202610141482 to 202610141490)

**Boundaries.** All years use the current (2024) district boundaries. Results are re-tabulated to the current districts:
- **2020:** VEST 2020 precincts, area-weighted into the current districts.
- **2024:** official county totals for districts made of whole counties. Elsewhere, precincts are area-weighted into the districts.

**2024 precincts.** These now come from The New York Times 2024 presidential precinct results and boundaries (github.com/nytimes/presidential-precinct-map-2024, NYT C-UDA non-commercial license, with credit). The NYT data is merged per county with the earlier Census VTD + MEDSL data, keeping whichever matches the official county total better. Alaska keeps the official Division of Elections file. Examples:
- Florida: 61 of 67 counties are now within 2% of the official totals (28 before). Manatee now has 71 precincts at 99.6% of the official total (61 precincts at 85% before).
- Alabama: 49 of 67 counties are now within 2% (no file before).
- Statewide files were added for CT, DE, HI, MA, MO, NJ, ND, RI and WI. CO, WY and NE were completed.

**How the districts are filled:**
- Whole counties use the official county totals.
- Split counties use precincts, area-weighted. Before that, each county's precincts must sum to 70–100% of the official total and cover at least 70% of the county's area. Precincts are scaled per party to the official county totals.
- Rows filled this way carry the flag `est` (≈ estimate).
- **Validation:** I checked against districts with known results, using the final thresholds, on 18,827 districts. Median total error is 0.71%, median Republican-share error is 0.06 points, and the 90th-percentile Republican-share error is 1.02 points.

## Town rows in list-all drill-down: 'no data' audit (build 1527)

**Root cause (Idaho and most states).** The town polygons exist for every listed town, and 2024 precinct data exists in every Idaho county. The town value was computed by summing the precincts whose *centroid* lies inside the town polygon. A town that is smaller than the precinct(s) it sits in (typical for rural Idaho, e.g. Council and New Meadows are inside 5 county-wide precincts) has no precinct centroid inside it, so it showed 'no data'. Kamiah only worked because a precinct centroid happened to fall inside the city.

**Fix.** When no precinct centroid is inside the town, the town's result is the real results of the precinct(s) that overlap it, weighted by the share of the *town's* area lying in each precinct (28x28 sample grid, at least half of the town's area must be covered by precinct polygons; otherwise it stays 'no data'). Shares are the area-weighted precinct shares. Counts are the overlapping precincts' totals pro-rated the same way, so they are an upper bound for the town (a precinct also holds voters outside the town). These rows carry the approximate mark (≈) in lists. Towns that contain at least one precinct centroid are unchanged (exact, no mark). No precinct or number is invented: no overlapping precinct means no data.

2020 is a separate gap: no 2020 precinct file exists for most Idaho counties (only Ada, Blaine, Bonner, Butte, Caribou, Kootenai have one), so 2020 and delta rows for towns elsewhere stay 'no data' until 2020 precinct data is added (same for the states below).

## County tier for school districts, state house and state senate (build 1530)
National list -> states -> **counties** -> districts of the selected type. A district is listed under every county it touches (24x24 sample grid over the district box; counties with under 3% of the district's sample points are not listed). Values are the district's **whole-district** numbers, identical to the map pop-up; a district that lies in more than one county is marked ≈ in each county list. County rows show the county's own results. Nothing is pro-rated or invented.
